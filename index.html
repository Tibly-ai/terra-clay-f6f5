Shop the Collection Our Story

Made by hand, one at a time

Every mug starts as a lump of stoneware clay on the wheel. It gets trimmed, dried, bisque fired, glazed by hand, then fired again to 2,200 degrees.

That process takes about two weeks from start to finish, and no two pieces come out quite the same. We think that's the point. A small chip in the glaze or a slight lean in a bowl's rim is proof it passed through someone's hands before it reached your table.

What we make

Three ongoing collections, restocked in small batches as the kiln allows.

Mugs & Cups

Weighted for a good grip, glazed in ash, clay, and iron tones.

Bowls & Plates

Everyday dinnerware built to stack, stand up to a dishwasher, and age well.

Vases & Vessels

Taller pieces thrown in stages, made for dried stems or fresh cut flowers.

From wheel to shelf

01

Throw

Clay is weighed, centered, and shaped by hand on the wheel.

02

Trim & Dry

Pieces rest for days, then get trimmed to finish their feet and lines.

03

Glaze

Each piece is dipped and brushed by hand in our own mixed glazes.

04

Fire

A slow kiln firing to stoneware temperature seals the piece for daily use.
